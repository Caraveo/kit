**Rex** is Kit's AI helper: a cheerful little puppy who knows Kit's x language and Diagrams. Open him with <kbd>8</kbd>, the **Rex** button in the viewport bar, or **Project → View → Rex**. He sits at the bottom of the center area.

## Signing in

Rex uses your Kit account. It's free to start, with credits each month.

1. Click **Sign in to Kit**, or just type a message and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.
2. Your browser opens and shows a short code. Check that it matches the one in Kit, then click **Connect**.
3. That's it: Kit stays signed in. Use **Sign out** under Rex to disconnect.

## Rex's modes

| Mode | What Rex does |
|---|---|
| **Buddy** | Answers questions about your game and Kit |
| **Fetch** | Writes a ready-to-use x script |
| **Sniff** | Finds bugs and explains errors |
| **Dig** | Builds a Diagram (visual logic, no code) |
| **Zoomies** | Brainstorms game ideas |
| **Sit & Learn** | Teaches step by step |

Each mode keeps its own conversation.

## Using what Rex makes

- **Scripts:** Kit checks every script with its own parser. If it parses, click **Add to *object*** to save it in `scripts/`, attach it to the selected object and open it in the code editor.
- **Diagrams:** Kit validates every Diagram. If it's valid, click **Use on *object*** to save it in `diagrams/`, attach it and open the Diagram view.
- **Copy** puts any code block on the clipboard.

**Share scene** (on by default) sends Rex a short summary of your project: the objects in the scene, the selected object and its script, and recent console errors. That way he can answer about *your* game. Turn it off to send only your message.

**Ask Rex** appears next to errors in the console. It opens Rex in Sniff mode with the error filled in.

## Credits

Each answer costs a few credits, based on its length (most cost 1–5). Your balance shows under Rex. When you run out, Rex links to the pricing page for more.
